## Equipment: Blast Chiller / Freezer

**Used for:** Setting mousse and cheesecake components before flocage/glazing, rapid-cooling compotes and jellies before use.
**Typical setting:** -18°C to -35°C depending on unit
**Note:** Cheesecake and similar mousse-based items must be fully frozen solid before flocage spraying or the pink flocage won't set correctly on the surface.

---

## Equipment: Spray Gun / Flocage Booth

**Used for:** Velvet/flocage finish on frozen cakes (e.g. Vanilla & Strawberry Cheesecake).
**Setting:** Chocolate/cocoa butter mixture melted and sprayed at 30°C (per cheesecake recipe card).
**Note:** Item being sprayed must be frozen, not just chilled, or the flocage will not create the correct textured finish.
