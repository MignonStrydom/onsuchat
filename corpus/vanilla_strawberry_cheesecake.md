## Recipe: Vanilla & Strawberry Cheesecake (香草草莓蛋糕)

**Category:** Patisserie / individual cake
**Finished weight:** 115g
**Allergens:** Cereals containing gluten, eggs, fish, dairy

### Assembly & finishing
Built in a 55g Charlotte silicone mould with a 5cm acrylic disc:
1. Pipe the set cheesecake mix (55g) into the Charlotte mould.
2. Add strawberry compote (20g) using a 3.5cm round mould insert.
3. Add the digestive base.
4. Spray with pink flocage at 30°C for the exterior finish.
5. Splatter red cocoa butter using a toothbrush for texture.
6. Warm strawberry jelly gently (do not overheat) and set 10g in the cavity.
7. Finish with fresh fruit: 1 strawberry (cut into 4 quarters), 1 pineberry (cut into 4 quarters), 1 strass berry (cut into 2 halves), and nappage the cut fruit.

### Component: Set Cheesecake Mix (yields 11,111g batch)
- Cream cheese: 8,621g
- Icing sugar: 2,490g
- Gelatin mass: 600g
- UHT milk: 1,916g
- Vanilla: to taste (quantity not specified on source card)

Method: Warm the UHT milk and gelatin together. Separately, smooth the Philadelphia cream cheese with the icing sugar. Add the hot UHT/gelatin mix into the cream cheese mixture and combine until smooth. Pipe 55g into charlotte moulds. 

### Component: Digestive Base (yields 1,500g batch)
- Digestive biscuits: 1,000g
- Butter: 500g
- Salt: 8g

Method: Blitz the digestive biscuits to a fine crumb. Add semi-melted butter and salt, mix to combine.

### Component: Strawberry Compote (yields 2,495g batch)
- Frozen strawberries: 2,020g
- Pectin NH: 25g
- Sugar: 250g
- Sosa Oligofruit: 200g

Method: Warm the strawberries to 40°C. Mix the pectin and sugar together, then add to the warmed liquid. Bring to the boil and reduce to Brix 35.

### Component: Strawberry Consommé (yields 5,400g batch)
- Frozen strawberries: 5,000g
- Icing sugar: 400g

Method: Steam at 90°C for 2 hours.

### Component: Strawberry Jelly (yields 1,450g batch)
- Strawberry consommé: 1,250g
- Gelatin mass: 200g
- Sugar: 125g

Method: Warm the strawberry consommé to 40°C. Mix the pectin and sugar, add to the warmed liquid, bring to the boil, then add the gelatin.

### Component: Pink Flocage (yields 2,800g batch)
- Ivoire (white chocolate): 1,750g
- Coconut oil: 1,000g
- Red cocoa butter: 50g

Method: Melt to 30°C. Spray evenly onto the frozen cake for the velvet finish.

### Notes / common mistakes
Strawberry jelly must be warmed gently and never overheated before setting in the cavity, or it will melt the cheesecake. Allow to set properly. 
Mousse must be frozen solid from blast before spraying or it will not spray properly
Compote must be placed as level and centre as possible
Pink Flocage must be sprayed at 30C, too hot and it results in a shiny appearance not velvet. 
Move the cheesecakes onto cards BEFORE filling the jelly otherwise it will spill. 
Strawberry tops need to be removed by carving out with a knife, not cutting straight across.
