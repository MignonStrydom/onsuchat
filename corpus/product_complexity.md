# ONSU Product components & Complexity
This file contains the individual recipes that build the final products for sale. Each component corresponds to a recipe in the recipe_costings and recipe_formulations files. They are used to calculate individual product cost and in turn compare ingredient margins and contribution to profit against selling price in the product_spec file. 
The Complexity refers to the number of recipes in the final product, high complexity generally results in higher labour costs due to additional steps needed in the production process.
