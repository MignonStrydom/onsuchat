# ONSU Baking Times & Oven Settings

Oven type, temperature, fan setting, humidity, and timing for ONSU's baked/steamed items, sourced from the production baking-times reference and the service/à-la-minute baking-times reference.

**Deck oven items** have their top and bottom heating elements set independently and running *simultaneously* (not sequential stages) — these are recorded as "Top element" / "Bottom element". Any additional time/humidity detail (e.g. spray water then steam, or a check-and-continue time split) describes the technique through that one bake, not a change in oven setting.

**Rational, steam, and deep-fry entries** genuinely do change temperature/fan/humidity over the course of the bake (e.g. Cannele steps down from 210°C to 150°C) — these are recorded as sequential numbered stages.

"Production" times are for the make/bake stage; "Service" times are for items baked or finished fresh at point of sale.
