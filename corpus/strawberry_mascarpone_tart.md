## Recipe: Strawberry Mascarpone Tart 草莓塔

**Category:** Patisserie / individual cake
**Finished weight:** 145g
**Allergens:** Cereals containing gluten, eggs, fish, dairy, nuts (almond)

### Assembly & finishing
1. Cut Sweetpaste into 3.5x25cm strips, 25g per portion
2. Blind Bake 160C 15mins fan3. Check colour and add more time if needed. 
3. Spray Tart shell with Egg wash mix. Bake 5mins 160C fan 3
4. Brush inside tart with Ivoire white chocolate. Allow to set
5. Fill shell with mascarpone cream, form slight indent in centre with palette knife. 
6. Remove green top from strawberry, do not cut straight off. Cut strawberries in half
7. Arrange 7x halves overlapping inside tart edge with cut side facing inwards. 
8. Add 2x more halves on top of tart. 
9. Put tarts close together on tray, spray with neutral nappage. 
10. Dip mini strawberry in red nappage. place inside cavity created by strawberries. 
11. Finish with logo

### Component: Almond Sweetpaste (yields 8,000g batch)
- Soft Flour:	3,456 
- Butter:	2,056
- Almond Powder:	414
- Salt:	18
- Icing Sugar:	1,264 
- Whole Egg:	774

Method: Mix the flour and butter together to a sand, add all dry ingredients, mix. Add eggs and mix to a minimum. Rest overnight.
Next day, divide into rectangular pieces to make rolling easier. Roll using flour to 1.5mm thick. Roll sheets 30x50cm minimum dimensions. 
Cut into 2.5x25cm strips. Line tart rings. Freeze before baking. 

### Component:  Mascarpone Cream (yields 4,968g)
Mascarpone:	3,200g
UHT:	800g
Sugar: 800g
Vanilla Pod:	8g
Gelatin mass:	160g

Method: Warm Cream to dissolve sugar. Add gelatin. Do not boil. Combine little with mascarpone before smoothing all. Set overnight

### Component: Egg Wash (yields 700g)
- Whipping Cream:	100g
- Egg yolk:	600g

Method: Combine all 

### Component: Red Nappage
- Absolu Cristal: 1000g
- Water: 100g
- Red water soluble colour: 2g

Method: Dissovle colour in a little water. Blend into remaning ingredients. 

### Notes / common mistakes
Sweetpaste must be rolled from the fridge, otherwise it gets sticky. 
Sweetpaste must be rolled using flour and not between paper. Specified dimensions are non-negotiable. 
Mascarpone cream splits if heated too much, make sure everything is just warm enough to melt.
Gelatin mass needs to be melted seperately
Strawberry tops need to be removed by carving out with a knife, not cutting straight across. 
Nappage can be sprayed generously. 
Red Colouring needs to be dissolved before adding to avoid lumps and uneven distribution. 
Neutral nappage needs to be boiled before spraying otherwise the spray gun clogs.
