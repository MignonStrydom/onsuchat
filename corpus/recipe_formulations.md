# ONSU Recipe Component Formulations & Batch Yields

Full ingredient breakdowns and batch-yield data for ONSU's recipe components, sourced from the production formulation and batch-yield sheets. Quantities are in grams unless noted otherwise. "Batch yield" shows how many portions/units a full batch produces, and the amount used per unit. These components are combined into finished menu items — see product_spec.md for how each component is used, and costings.md for per-component cost.

**Data quality notes:**
- "Curry Beef Filling" has two documented batch sizes (2,585g and 14,520g); the ingredient breakdown given matches the 14,520g batch. The 2,585g batch's ingredient list at that scale isn't in the source data — assume it's the same ratios scaled down unless told otherwise.
- A handful of recipes appear in the batch-yield sheet with no matching ingredient breakdown (e.g. Strawberry Compote/Jelly/Consommé, Raspberry Veil, Vanilla Madeleine Mix, Salted Yolk Doughnut Filling, Salted Yolk Pastry Cream, Sesame Seaweed Mix, Teriyaki Chicken Filling) — these are marked "ingredient breakdown not recorded here" below. Some of these (e.g. Strawberry Compote/Jelly) already have method steps documented elsewhere in the corpus.
- Repeated ingredient names within one recipe (e.g. two additions of butter or flour at different stages) are labelled "(2nd addition)", "(3rd addition)", etc. in the order they appeared in the source sheet.
