## Recipe: Basque Cheesecake (巴斯克起司蛋糕)


**Category:** Patisserie / individual cake
**Finished weight:** 220g
**Allergens:** eggs, dairy

### Assembly & finishing
1. Pour mix into lined 3" tin 200g
2. Blowtorch top to form a skin
3. Bake 0% humidity fan5 240C 8mins
4. 2x trays at a time, 12x per rack (3x4 arrangement)
5. 2nd shelf from top, 3rd from bottom.
6. Rest to room temperature before moving to fridge
7. Place in 3" Baroque Cake box
8. Label with today's date for selling. 

### Component: Basque Cheesecake Mix (yields 18,526g)
- Cream Cheese:	8,565g
- Sugar:	2,516g 
- Cornflour:	240g 
- Salt:	18g
- Egg Yolk:	1,198g
- Whole Egg:	2,396g
- Whipping Cream:	3,594g

Method:
Blitz all in biggest campbro container using Large hand blender. Strain. Chill before baking. 

### Notes / common mistakes
Don't overload racks as overcrowding will result in uneven colouring
Do not burn the paper with the blowtorch, will be unsellable
Can keep for 3 days if unsold. 
Make sure oven is hot before baking. Allow to come to temperature again before baking next batch. 
Make sure shelf height remains the same to avoid uneven colouring
Cakes HAVE to cool to room temperature before moving to the fridge to allow latent heat to cook the inside. 
Trays in fridge must be labelled with today's date to avoid batch mixups upstairs.
