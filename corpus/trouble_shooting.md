## Troubleshooting: Cheesecake / Mousse Won't Set

**Symptom:** Cheesecake mix stays soft or runny after freezing, or collapses after unmoulding.
**Likely causes:**
- Gelatin mass not fully bloomed or added at the wrong temperature (should be added to a hot liquid, not straight into cold cream cheese).
- Incorrect gelatin ratio for the batch size.
- Blast chiller not reaching target temperature or item removed before fully set.
**Fix:** Confirm gelatin mass is fully melted before combining with the UHT/cream cheese mix, and confirm the item spent the full recommended time in the blast chiller before unmoulding.

## Troubleshooting: Flocage / Velvet Spray Not Adhering Correctly

**Symptom:** Pink flocage looks patchy, drips, or doesn't create the velvet texture.
**Likely causes:**
- Cake wasn't fully frozen (flocage only works correctly on a frozen surface — condensation forms on a chilled-but-not-frozen surface and ruins the finish).
- Spray mixture too hot or too cold (target ~30°C).
- Spray gun held too close, causing pooling instead of a fine mist.
**Fix:** Confirm cake is fully frozen solid, confirm mixture is at 30°C, and hold spray gun at the correct distance (CONFIRM: house standard distance, typically 20-30cm).

## Troubleshooting: Jelly/Glaze Layer Cloudy or Grainy

**Symptom:** Strawberry jelly sets cloudy or with visible undissolved sugar/pectin.
**Likely causes:**
- Pectin and sugar not mixed together before adding to warm liquid (pectin added directly to liquid will clump).
- Liquid overheated, breaking down the pectin's setting ability.
**Fix:** Always pre-mix pectin with sugar dry before adding to warm liquid, and never let the jelly mixture boil harder than a gentle simmer.
