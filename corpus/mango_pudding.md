## Recipe: Onsu Mango pudding (芒果布丁)


**Category:** Patisserie / individual cake
**Finished weight:** 130
**Allergens:** eggs, dairy

### Assembly & finishing
1. 50g of mango compote in bottom of glass. Freeze solid. Can make a few days stock in advance. 
2. Pour mango pudding mix to just below lip of glass. 80g. Only make for the next day. 
3. Allow to set

### Component: Mango compote (yields 1,600g)
- Frozen Mango:	1100g
- Mango Puree: 500g

Method: Dice mango into big chunks. Mix with Puree

### Component: Mango Pudding (yields 2538g)
- Mango Puree:	1125g
- Fromage Frais:	225g
- Coconut Puree:	900g
- Whole Milk:	225g
- Sugar:	208g
- Gelatin Mass:	112g
- Fresh Whipping Cream:	562g

Method: Melt Mango & Coconut Puree. 
Blitz till smooth. 
Cook milk, whipping cream and sugar to 40C. 
Add Gelatin. 
Blitz into puree
Blitz melted Fromage Frais

### Notes / common mistakes
Disinfect glasses in oven if straight from box
Mango compote needs to be frozen solid before pouring pudding to avoid disrupting the compote
Mango pudding needs to be blitzed thoroughly
Frozen mango is easier to cut from frozen. Check for offcuts from other recipes to add to the compote
All purees can be defrosted the day before they are needed to save time.
