# ONSU Product Cost & Margin

Per-menu-item cost breakdown and profit margin: ingredient cost, packaging cost, total variable cost (TVC), selling price, contribution to profit, and contribution %. Sourced from the product costing/margin sheet. This is menu-item-level margin data — distinct from costings.md (per-component batch cost) and recipe_formulations.md (ingredient breakdowns).

**Data quality note:** three items — Jasmine and Mango Panna Cotta, Caramelised Pineapple Tart, Strawberry Tart — show £0.00 (blank) ingredient cost and a 0% contribution figure in the source sheet, but their stated Contribution to Profit (£4.08–£6.66) is clearly positive and close to the full selling price. The 0% figure is almost certainly a spreadsheet formula error (likely a divide-by-zero from the blank ingredient cost cell) rather than a real 0% margin — flagged rather than corrected, since the true ingredient cost isn't available to calculate a real percentage. Do not treat these three items as having no profit margin.
